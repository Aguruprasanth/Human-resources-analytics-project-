# Human-resources-analytics-project-
Analysis about the customer turnover and retention 
## Project Description

**Employee Turnover and Retention Analytics**

This project focuses on analysing employee turnover and retention patterns in an organisation. The main objective is to identify the major factors that influence employee attrition and provide insights that can help improve employee retention.

A dataset of 1,000 employee records was prepared for analysis. The data was **not obtained from any company’s official HR system or company records**. Instead, it is **rough/simulated data** created based on realistic workforce assumptions, including employee age, gender, department, job role, education, experience, tenure, salary, job satisfaction, work-life balance, working hours, overtime, training, promotions, mentorship, and turnover status.

The dataset was cleaned, organized, and analyzed using **Python**. Exploratory data analysis was performed to understand turnover patterns across departments, job roles, age groups, experience levels, and other employee-related factors. The analysis examined whether factors such as low job satisfaction, poor work-life balance, high overtime, limited career development, lack of training, and absence of promotions are associated with employee turnover.

Based on the analysis, employees were grouped into turnover-risk categories. The project also suggests possible retention measures, such as improving work-life balance, providing career development opportunities, offering training programs, introducing mentorship support, and monitoring employee workload and overtime.

Since the dataset is simulated, the results are intended for learning, research, and demonstration purposes. They do not represent the actual employee data or official turnover statistics of any company. However, the project demonstrates the complete process of HR analytics, including data preparation, analysis, turnover-risk identification, and recommendation of retention strategies.
